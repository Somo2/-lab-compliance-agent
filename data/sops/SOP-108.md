# SOP-108: Environmental Monitoring — Temperature and Humidity

**Revision:** 2 | **Effective Date:** 2025-01-10 | **Author:** Sarah Chen  
**Department:** Quality Assurance | **Classification:** GxP-Supporting

## 1. Purpose

Defines environmental monitoring requirements for laboratory and storage areas.

## 2. Scope

All controlled laboratory spaces, sample storage areas, and instrument rooms.

## 3. Monitoring Requirements

### 3.1 Laboratory Areas

| Parameter | Acceptable Range | Alert Limit | Action Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Temperature | 20.0 – 25.0 | 19.0 / 26.0 | 18.0 / 27.0 |
| Relative Humidity | 30 – 60 | 25 / 65 | 20 / 70 |

### 3.2 Cold Storage (2–8°C Refrigerators)

| Parameter | Acceptable Range | Alert Limit | Action Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Temperature | 2.0 – 8.0 | 1.0 / 9.0 | 0.0 / 10.0 |

### 3.3 Freezer Storage (-25 to -15°C)

| Parameter | Acceptable Range | Alert Limit | Action Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Temperature | -25.0 – -15.0 | -27.0 / -13.0 | -30.0 / -10.0 |

## 4. Monitoring Frequency

- Continuous monitoring via validated data loggers.
- Manual verification: twice daily (AM and PM readings).
- Data logger download: weekly.

## 5. Excursion Handling

### 5.1 Alert Limit Excursion

1. Notify Lab Supervisor within 1 hour.
2. Document in Environmental Monitoring Log.
3. Assess impact on stored materials.

### 5.2 Action Limit Excursion

1. Immediately relocate affected samples to compliant storage.
2. Initiate Deviation Report per SOP-010.
3. Perform impact assessment on all affected samples/reagents.
4. Quality Assurance review required before resuming use of affected materials.

## 6. Records

Retain monitoring data for minimum 5 years.
