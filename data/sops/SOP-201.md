# SOP-201: Buffer Preparation for pH Calibration

**Revision:** 3 | **Effective Date:** 2025-03-15 | **Author:** Dr. Elena Vasquez  
**Department:** Analytical Chemistry | **Classification:** GxP-Critical

## 1. Purpose

Defines the procedure for preparing pH calibration buffers used in QC testing.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all pH buffer preparations in the Analytical Chemistry QC lab.

## 3. Materials

- Potassium hydrogen phthalate (KHP), ACS grade
- Sodium phosphate dibasic, ACS grade
- Purified water (Type I, resistivity ≥ 18.2 MΩ·cm)

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Preparation

1. Weigh reagents to ± 0.1 mg using calibrated analytical balance.
2. Dissolve in 900 mL purified water at 25 ± 2°C.
3. Adjust volume to 1000 mL in volumetric flask.

### 4.2 Acceptance Criteria

| Parameter | Acceptable Range | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| pH (at 25°C) | 7.35 – 7.45 | - |
| Conductivity | 1200 – 1800 | µS/cm |
| Temperature | 23.0 – 27.0 | °C |
| Visual clarity | Clear, colorless | - |

### 4.3 Documentation

Record all measurements in the Buffer Preparation Log (Form QC-201-F1).

Any out-of-specification result requires initiation of a Deviation Report per SOP-010.

## 5. Storage

Store prepared buffers at 2–8°C. Shelf life: 30 days from preparation date.
